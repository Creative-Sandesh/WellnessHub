Tips for Healthy Eating

Simple strategies to improve your nutrition habits

Eat the Rainbow

Include a variety of colorful fruits and vegetables in your diet. Different colors provide different nutrients and antioxidants.

Plan Your Meals

Take time to plan your weekly meals and snacks. This helps you make healthier choices and saves money.

Stay Hydrated

Drink plenty of water throughout the day. Aim for 8-10 glasses daily to maintain energy and focus.

Portion Control

Be mindful of portion sizes. Use smaller plates and listen to your body's hunger and fullness cues.

Choose Whole Foods

Opt for whole, unprocessed foods over packaged items. They're typically more nutritious and filling.

Cook at Home

Prepare meals at home when possible. You'll have better control over ingredients and portion sizes.
